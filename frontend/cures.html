Diabetic Retinopathy Cures

Introduction

Diabetic retinopathy can be managed with timely treatment, reducing the risk of vision loss. Treatment varies based on the severity of the condition. Early detection and proper care can help maintain eyesight.

Lifestyle Management (Early Stage)

  • Blood Sugar Control: Maintain stable sugar levels to prevent further damage.
  • Blood Pressure & Cholesterol Management: Reducing hypertension slows disease progression.
  • Healthy Diet & Exercise: Helps maintain overall eye health.
  • Regular Eye Checkups: Detects early changes and prevents complications.

Laser Treatment (For Moderate Cases)

Laser therapy is used to prevent vision loss by targeting abnormal blood vessels.

  • Focal Laser Therapy: Seals leaking blood vessels to stop fluid accumulation.
  • Scatter Laser Treatment: Prevents the growth of new abnormal blood vessels.

Injections (For Advanced Cases)

Medications injected into the eye reduce swelling and slow abnormal blood vessel growth.

  • Anti-VEGF Injections: Reduce swelling and prevent new vessel formation.
  • Steroid Injections: Help control inflammation but may have side effects.

Vitrectomy (For Severe Cases)

Surgical removal of blood and scar tissue inside the eye to restore vision.

Experimental & Emerging Treatments

New research is exploring advanced treatments:

  • Gene Therapy: Investigating genetic factors to prevent disease progression.
  • Artificial Retina Technology: Researching devices to restore lost vision.
